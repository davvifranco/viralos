Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

ViralOS (“we”, “us”, or “our”) provides a workspace that helps creators plan, manage, and prepare short-form video content. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use ViralOS.

Information we may process

Depending on the features you choose to use, ViralOS may process account information you provide, content and media you upload or prepare, and information made available by a social-platform account after you explicitly authorize a connection.

When you connect a TikTok account, ViralOS only requests and uses permissions necessary for the features you choose to activate, such as preparing or publishing content and viewing information made available through the approved TikTok permissions. We do not receive your TikTok password.

How we use information

We use information to provide and improve ViralOS, operate requested features, protect the service, communicate with you about the service, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing and retention

We share information only with service providers that help operate ViralOS, with social platforms when you direct us to use a connected feature, when required by law, or as part of a business transfer. We retain information only for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Your choices

You can disconnect a connected social account at any time through ViralOS or the social platform’s account settings. You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate operational requirements.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as ViralOS evolves. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at davvifsantos@gmail.com.
